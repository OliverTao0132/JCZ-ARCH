Luogo Di Venezia

This project is an affordable housing initiative situated in Venice, CA. The design concept draws inspiration from the wooden piles of the boat docks in Venice, Italy. These elements are reimagined through transformation and compression, resulting in unique and intriguing forms. The design is simple yet rich with intricate details that invite contemplation and curiosity.
